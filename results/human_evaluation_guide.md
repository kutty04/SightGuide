# SightGuide Human Evaluation Protocol & Reviewer Guidelines

## 1. Study Overview
This human evaluation assesses image-caption quality generated by **SightGuide**, an image-captioning system extending Celona et al. (2025), *Improving Image Captioning Descriptiveness by Ranking and LLM-Based Fusion* (Springer).

The primary research comparison evaluates:
- **Control Method**: Naive CLIP Top-2 candidate selection ($\lambda = 1.0$) followed by LLM caption fusion.
- **Proposed Method**: Diversity-Aware candidate-pair selection ($\lambda = 0.5$) balancing visual grounding and semantic complementarity followed by identical LLM caption fusion.

To eliminate evaluator bias, the evaluation is **double-blinded**:
- Candidate methods are labeled anonymously as **Method A** and **Method B**.
- Assignments are randomized 50/50 across benchmark images.
- Evaluators are provided with the source image, the regenerated BLIP baseline caption, and the two candidate captions.

---

## 2. Fundamental Evaluation Rules & Caveats

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Crucial Evaluation Rule**:
> **Greater caption length, higher diversity, or larger vocabulary does NOT automatically imply higher quality or factual accuracy.**
> A caption that adds unsupported objects, incorrect spatial relations, or hallucinations must receive a **low rating**, regardless of how fluent or descriptive it appears.

1. **Grounding First**: Every descriptive detail must be verifiable in the source image located at `image_path` (e.g. `dataset/<image>`).
2. **Conservative Fusion**: LLM-fused captions are instructed never to invent objects or speculative actions. Penalize unsupported claims heavily.
3. **Handle Corruptions & Artifacts**: Watch out for known model failure modes:
   - Invented entities (e.g., claiming a "kite" in cloud photos).
   - Word fragments or token truncations (e.g., "bum" instead of "bee").
   - Hallucinated interactions between entities.

## 3. Rating Dimensions & 1–5 Scoring Rubric

Each candidate caption (Method A and Method B) must be scored independently on an integer scale from **1 to 5** across four orthogonal dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Accuracy & Visual Grounding (1–5)
*Evaluates whether all described objects, attributes, actions, and locations are factually present in the image.*
- **5 (Excellent)**: Every entity, attribute, and relationship mentioned is completely accurate and verifiable.
- **4 (Good)**: Major entities and scene context are accurate; minor non-critical imprecision (e.g., slightly ambiguous color or background descriptor).
- **3 (Fair)**: Partially accurate; at least one entity or action is misidentified or questionable, but the main subject is correct.
- **2 (Poor)**: Significant inaccuracies; misidentifies primary subjects, attributes, or actions.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Completely inaccurate; describes an entirely different scene or fabricates core subjects.

### Dimension 2: Relevant Descriptive Detail (1–5)
*Evaluates whether the caption provides meaningful, salient visual information without fluff, repetition, or irrelevant verbosity.*
- **5 (Excellent)**: Rich, salient visual details (key foreground subjects, background context, spatial arrangement, distinctive attributes).
- **4 (Good)**: Sufficient descriptive detail capturing main subjects and key surroundings; minor omission of secondary details.
- **3 (Fair)**: Minimally descriptive; states only obvious high-level categories (e.g., "a dog on the ground") with little descriptive depth.
- **2 (Poor)**: Too sparse to be helpful, or padded with redundant words that add no visual information.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Completely devoid of useful visual information or purely uninformative.

### Dimension 3: Hallucination Control (1–5)
*Evaluates the strict absence of fabricated objects, phantom entities, or corrupted tokens.*
- **5 (No Hallucination)**: Zero hallucinated entities, attributes, actions, or corrupted words.
- **4 (Minimal / Minor)**: No invented entities, but contains minor speculative phrasing (e.g., guessing an indoor setting when ambiguous).
- **3 (Noticeable Speculation)**: Mentions a plausible but non-verifiable entity or attribute (e.g., "in a garden" when only a flower is visible).
- **2 (Substantial Hallucination)**: Fabricates a distinct object or action that is clearly not present in the image.
- **1 (Severe Hallucination / Corruption)**: Severe entity hallucination (e.g., "flying a kite" in an empty sky) or corrupted tokens (e.g., "bum on flower").

### Dimension 4: Overall Caption Quality (1–5)
*Holistic synthesis of fluency, naturalness, conciseness, and accessibility for visually impaired users.*
- **5 (Excellent)**: Clear, natural, fluent, concisely structured, and highly informative for accessibility.
- **4 (Good)**: Fluent and easy to understand with good accessibility value; minor syntactic awkwardness.
- **3 (Fair)**: Comprehensible but awkwardly phrased, slightly repetitive, or unbalanced in focus.
- **2 (Poor)**: Disjointed phrasing, difficult to understand, or low accessibility utility.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Incomprehensible, severely corrupted, or misleading.

## 4. Evaluator Workflow

1. Open `results/sightguide_human_eval_blinded_template.csv` in a spreadsheet editor (e.g., Excel, LibreOffice Calc, or Google Sheets).
2. For each row:
   - Check `data_quality_status`:
     - If `READY_FOR_EVALUATION`: Proceed to rate Method A and Method B.
     - If `PENDING_LIVE_GENERATION`: **DO NOT RATE**. These records await live API execution and will be evaluated once generated.
     - If `EXCLUDED_HISTORICAL_FAILURE`: **DO NOT RATE**. The model generated an empty response historically.
   - Open the image file specified in `image_path` (e.g., `dataset/anna-tukhfatullina-...jpg`).
   - Read the regenerated BLIP baseline caption for context (`baseline_caption_regenerated`).
   - Carefully inspect `method_a_caption` and `method_b_caption`.
   - Score Method A from 1 to 5 across the 4 dimensions.
   - Score Method B from 1 to 5 across the 4 dimensions.
   - Optionally add brief reviewer notes in `reviewer_comments` (e.g., noted missing object, identified hallucinated word).
3. Save the completed ratings CSV (e.g. as `results/sightguide_human_eval_completed_r1.csv`).

## 5. Automated Analysis Execution

Once reviewer ratings are saved, run the analysis utility:

```powershell
python D:\SightGuide\experiments\analyze_human_evaluation.py `
  --ratings-csv D:\SightGuide\results\sightguide_human_eval_completed_r1.csv `
  --blinding-key-csv D:\SightGuide\results\sightguide_human_eval_blinding_key.csv `
  --output-details-csv D:\SightGuide\results\sightguide_human_eval_analysis_details.csv `
  --output-summary-csv D:\SightGuide\results\sightguide_human_eval_analysis_summary.csv
```

This utility automatically unblinds Method A and Method B to Control and Proposed, excludes unexecuted/failed records, checks reviewer bounds and consistency, computes paired differences ($\Delta = \text{Proposed} - \text{Control}$), aggregates multi-reviewer ratings without unit-of-analysis inflation, and produces audit-ready summary tables.
